SubAddress Generator — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

SubAddress Generator is a browser extension that helps you create unique email subaddresses (plus addressing) for testing websites and applications.

Data collection

We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal data with us or any third party. The extension has no backend, analytics, telemetry, or accounts.

Data stored on your device

The extension uses chrome.storage.local only on your computer to save:

  • Your primary email, prefix, suffix, and last selected template (optional convenience)
  • Custom templates you create
  • Recent generated email strings (only if you enable history)

This data never leaves your browser except when you copy an address to the clipboard yourself.

Permissions

  • storage — save settings and optional history locally
  • activeTab — read the current tab URL when generating (for template rules)
  • contextMenus — “Generate email” on form fields
  • Content scripts on web pages — show a helper control near email fields and insert or copy generated addresses when you ask. No page content is sent to any server.

Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue or contact the developer through the extension’s Chrome Web Store listing support link.
